Most transformation efforts fail for the same reason: organizations build more capacity to act without building the capacity to decide together.

I work on the second problem — designing the conversations, methods, and platforms that let industries, agencies, and communities think and commit as one system instead of a pile of competing agendas.

For three decades that has meant enterprise product work at scale, founding and running companies, and inventing tools for collaborative sense-making. Today it means putting all of it to work on collaboration as infrastructure.

Now

OnTrackNorthAmerica / OnTrackWorld

Chief Innovation Officer  ·  July 2015–Present  ·  Philadelphia, PA

OnTrackNorthAmerica is a 501(c)(3) action think tank working to redesign how North America plans, capitalizes, and builds its industrial and freight systems — expanding rail utilization, integrating freight with land use, and replacing siloed planning with coordinated, whole-system decision-making. OTNA has held a thirty-year pledge of impartiality: it takes no sponsorship that would prioritize one party's agenda over shared interests, which is what makes genuinely level-playing-field dialogue possible.

The organization's core method is IntelliSynthesis® — a structured approach to collaborative problem-solving that gets impacted and influential parties thinking and acting together fast, so that posturing and narrow vested interests fall away rather than dominate. It runs through IntelliConference events, facilitated stakeholder dialogues, and Transportation Action Planning engagements with governments, railroads, shippers, and communities.
